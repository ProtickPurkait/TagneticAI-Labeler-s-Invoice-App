Background

The Company provides artificial-intelligence data services, including data labelling, annotation, video review and related work, for itself and for its clients. The Employee has been engaged by the Company and, in the course of that engagement, will receive or have access to Confidential Information. The parties therefore agree as follows.

1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information, in any form, that the Employee receives or accesses in connection with the engagement, including:

(a) client data, datasets, images, videos, audio, text and documents provided for labelling, annotation or review;

(b) labels, annotations, reviews, outputs and all other work product;

(c) annotation guidelines and instructions, project names, the identity of clients, and the existence and nature of any project;

(d) software, tools, platforms, models, prompts, source code, login credentials and access methods;

(e) pricing, rates, payment terms, business plans, and information about other employees or contractors; and

(f) any personal data relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

2. Exclusions

Clause 3 does not apply to information that the Employee can show (a) is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement, (b) was lawfully known to the Employee before the engagement without any duty of confidence, or (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it. If the Employee is required by law or by a court or government authority to disclose Confidential Information, the Employee shall, where lawful, promptly inform the Company and disclose only the minimum required.

3. Obligations of the Employee

The Employee shall:

(a) use Confidential Information only to perform the work assigned by the Company;

(b) not disclose Confidential Information to anyone other than the Company's authorised personnel who need to know it;

(c) not copy, download, screenshot, photograph, screen-record, print or otherwise reproduce Confidential Information except as expressly required by the assigned work;

(d) not upload, paste or submit Confidential Information to any third-party service, including public AI tools and chatbots, translation services, file-sharing services or personal cloud storage, unless the Company has approved it in writing;

(e) access the systems of the Company and its clients only with credentials issued to the Employee, keep those credentials secret, and never share an account;

(f) protect Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, including on any personal device used for work; and

(g) inform the Company immediately on becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorised access, use or disclosure.

4. Personal Data

Datasets may contain personal data such as faces, voices, vehicle registration numbers or other identifiers. The Employee shall process such data only as instructed by the Company, shall not attempt to identify, contact or profile any individual, and shall comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the rules made under them, as applicable.

5. Ownership

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Company or its clients. All labels, annotations, reviews and other work product created by the Employee in the course of the engagement belong exclusively to the Company (or to its client, as the Company directs). Nothing in this Agreement grants the Employee any licence or right in them.

6. Return and Deletion

When a project ends, when the engagement ends for any reason, or whenever the Company asks, the Employee shall within seven (7) days return or permanently delete all Confidential Information in the Employee's possession or control, including copies on personal devices and accounts, and shall confirm this in writing if asked.

7. Duration

This Agreement takes effect on the date above. The Employee's obligations continue during the engagement and for years after it ends, except that obligations concerning personal data and trade secrets continue for as long as that information remains personal data or a trade secret.

8. Breach

The Employee acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the Company and its clients irreparable harm. In addition to any other remedy available under law, including damages and termination of the engagement, the Company may seek an injunction to prevent or stop any breach.

9. Accuracy of Details

The Employee confirms that the personal details set out above were entered by the Employee and are true and correct, and shall inform the Company promptly of any change.

10. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it. It is the entire agreement between the parties on confidentiality and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties. If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest of the Agreement remains in force. A delay in enforcing any right is not a waiver of it. This Agreement does not by itself create or change any term of the Employee's engagement.
