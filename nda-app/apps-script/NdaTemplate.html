1. Definition of Confidential Information

For purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" shall include all information or material that has or could have commercial value or other utility in the business in which the Company is engaged. This includes, but is not limited to: source code, AI models, algorithms, machine learning datasets, technical specifications, business plans, financial data, client lists, marketing strategies, trade secrets, and any other proprietary information relating to the Company's current, future, and proposed products or services.

  • Operational Note: All data accessed through Company platforms and internal tools is proprietary and considered Confidential Information by default.

2. Obligations of Receiving Party

The Employee agrees to hold and maintain the Confidential Information in strictest confidence for the sole and exclusive benefit of the Company.

  • Access Control: The Employee shall carefully restrict access to Confidential Information to authorized personnel.
  • Usage Restrictions: The Employee shall not, without prior written approval of the Company, use for Employee's own benefit, publish, copy, or otherwise disclose to others any Confidential Information.
  • Portfolio Exception: The Employee may reference their role and general duties (e.g., "Video Data Annotator") on professional resumes or portfolios, provided no client identities or proprietary project details are revealed.

3. Exceptions to Confidential Information

The obligations under this Agreement do not apply to information that:

  • (a) is or becomes publicly known through no wrongful act of the Employee;
  • (b) is received from a third party without breach of any obligation of confidentiality;
  • (c) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided that the Employee gives the Company prompt written notice.

4. Intellectual Property and General Skills

The Employee acknowledges that all Confidential Information, including any modifications, enhancements, or derivatives thereof created during the course of employment, remains the sole and exclusive property of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting any rights to the Employee, by license or otherwise, to any Confidential Information.

  • Clarification of Skills: General skills, labeling techniques, and industry experience gained during the course of employment remain with the Employee and are not considered Confidential Information.

5. Return of Materials and Digital Backups

Upon the termination of employment or at the Company's request, the Employee shall immediately return all records, notes, and other written, printed, or tangible materials in their possession pertaining to Confidential Information.

  • Backup Exclusion: This clause shall not apply to Confidential Information residing on automatic, non-selective electronic backup systems, provided such information is not actively accessed or used after termination.

6. Term and Termination

The nondisclosure provisions regarding Trade Secrets shall survive the termination of the Employee's employment indefinitely. For all other Confidential Information, the obligations shall remain in effect for a fixed term of three (3) years following the termination of the Employee's employment.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Kolkata, West Bengal.
